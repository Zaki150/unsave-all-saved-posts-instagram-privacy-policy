Privacy Policy

Unsave All Saved Posts for Instagram (Chrome extension)
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unsave All Saved Posts for Instagram (“the Extension”) handles your information. The Extension works inside your browser. We do not collect, sell or share your Instagram account data or your saved posts.

1. Information the Extension Accesses

  • Website content: On instagram.com, the Extension reads the list of your own saved posts (post pictures, creator, type, date and post details) so it can show them to you and unsave the posts you select.
  • Authentication: The Extension uses your existing signed-in Instagram session to carry out the actions you request. It never reads, collects or stores your password.
  • License key: If you purchase the full version, the license key you enter is used to verify your purchase.
  • Support messages (optional): If you use “Report a problem”, the Extension sends the message you write, the email address you choose to give, and, if you leave the option on, basic technical details (extension version, browser type and error codes, with no usernames or posts).

2. How the Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Extension’s features: viewing, keeping, unsaving, downloading and exporting your saved posts, and replying to support requests you send. All processing of your Instagram data happens locally in your browser, and it is never sent to the developer.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Your saved posts list, pinned posts, settings, history, the free-use count and license status are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage. This data stays on your device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the Extension. Photos, videos and export files (CSV, JSON, TXT, HTML) you choose to download are saved directly to your own device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, except in these two cases, both started by you:

  • Gumroad: When you activate the full version, and about once a week afterwards, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, to confirm your purchase.
  • FormSubmit: When you send a “Report a problem” message, it is delivered to the developer’s email through the FormSubmit form service. It is used only to reply to you.

5. What We Never Do

  • Sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties.
  • Use data for advertising, profiling or to determine creditworthiness.
  • Use or transfer data for any purpose unrelated to the Extension’s single purpose.
  • Include analytics, tracking or remotely hosted code.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
